  **Optimization Strategy Applied (C)**

    - Reused a single `PeptidePropertiesImpl` and built output rows with `StringBuilder`.  
  - Second pass:  
    - Cached the amino acid compound list to avoid repeated `getAllCompounds()` allocations in `getAAComposition`.
